# Hero Text Options

Saved for reference. Pick one, or mix parts.

---

## Option A — Trade-off driven

> Good product work is mostly about what you choose not to build. I make that choice by understanding what people actually do, drawing clear boundaries where they matter, and shipping fast enough to find out if I was wrong.

---

## Option B — Behavior driven (CHOSEN)

> I care about what people actually do, not what they should do. That shapes every product decision: when to add friction, when to remove it, and when the best feature is the one you never build. I ship fast enough to find out if I was wrong.

---

## Option C — Systems driven

> I think in systems and trade-offs. Every product decision is a boundary: what belongs in v1, what stays out, where AI helps, where humans decide. The hard part is never the feature. It is the line.

---

## Why B was chosen

- Leads with user behavior, not internal process
- Avoids PM buzzwords (synergy, holistic, seamless, leverage)
- The "ship fast enough" ending adds credibility — it admits you might be wrong
- Works regardless of what projects are on the site
- Short enough for a hero, specific enough to remember
